# /grill-me

Use this command to challenge assumptions, gaps, contradictions, and scope-creep risks in a verbose or loosely planned request.

## Command

```text
/grill-me

Task: [PRODUCT OR RESEARCH DECISION]

Do not research.
Do not recommend.
Do not enter solution mode.

Challenge the assumptions, gaps, contradictions, and scope-creep risks in my request.

For each issue:
1. Quote or paraphrase the assumption
2. Explain why it may be wrong, incomplete, or risky
3. Identify the impact if it remains unchallenged
4. Ask the highest-leverage clarifying question
5. State what evidence or decision is required before proceeding

Focus on:
- Hidden assumptions
- Conflicting objectives
- Missing constraints
- Unverified specifications
- Safety and compatibility risks
- Budget and timing assumptions
- Scope creep
- Premature solution framing
- Professional-advice needs

End with:
- Assumption register
- Contradiction register
- Risk register
- Highest-leverage questions
- Proposed next gate
- What must be approved before research begins
```

## Gate

This command operates within G-2 and G-1. It must not advance to G0 without user approval.
