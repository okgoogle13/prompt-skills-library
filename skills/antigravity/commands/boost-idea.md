# /boost-idea

Use this command to expand a rough idea into a structured decision space without selecting a solution or beginning research.

## Command

```text
/boost-idea

Task: [ROUGH IDEA OR PRODUCT DECISION]

Do not research.
Do not recommend.
Do not enter solution mode.

Expand this idea into a structured decision space.

Produce:
1. Possible objectives
2. Candidate problem framings
3. Stakeholders and decision owners
4. Constraints and dependencies
5. Candidate solution categories
6. Trade-offs and risks
7. Scope-creep risks
8. Success measures
9. Reversibility options
10. Highest-leverage clarifying questions

Do not choose an option.
Do not begin research.
Do not produce a recommendation.

End with:
- A proposed problem definition
- A proposed gate plan
- The next prompt to run
- What I must approve before proceeding
```

## Gate

This command operates within G-2 and prepares G-1. It must not advance to G0 without user approval.
