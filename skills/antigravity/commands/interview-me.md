# /interview-me

Use this command to start a gated scoping interview for a new product, research, property, tax, renovation, or workflow decision.

## Command

```text
/interview-me

Task: [PRODUCT OR RESEARCH DECISION]
Project context: [PROJECT NAME]

Do not research.
Do not recommend.
Do not enter solution mode.

Run a gated scoping interview.

First, extract and classify everything I provide into:
1. Explicit facts
2. Direct inferences
3. Assumptions
4. Incorrect or risky assumptions
5. Contradictions
6. Missing information
7. Questions that can be answered now
8. Questions that require later research
9. Questions that require professional advice

Then ask only the highest-leverage questions needed to define the decision.

Interview sequence:
1. Problem and desired outcome
2. Constraints and non-negotiables
3. Budget, timing, and reversibility
4. Existing environment and compatibility
5. Safety, household, and cat-related constraints
6. Evidence and validation requirements
7. Decision criteria and priority weights
8. Scope boundaries and exclusions

Interview rules:
- Ask one cluster of questions at a time.
- Do not ask questions already answered.
- Do not ask broad questions when targeted questions will materially improve the brief.
- Separate facts, inferences, assumptions, and unknowns.
- Correct risky assumptions early.
- Identify conflicts, dependencies, and scope-creep risks.
- Do not proceed to research until I approve the research brief.

End with:
- Fact base
- Assumption register
- Unknown register
- Contradiction register
- Risk register
- Proposed next gate
- Exact information or approval required before that gate
```

## Gate

This command completes G-2 and prepares G-1. It must not advance to G0 without user approval.
