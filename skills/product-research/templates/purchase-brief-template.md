# Purchase Brief

Keep one separate `purchase-brief.md` inside each purchase folder. Never mix two purchases in one brief, chat, or workspace.

1. **Purchase ID & Date:** `[e.g. 2026-11-robot-vacuum]`
2. **Primary Job-to-Be-Done:** `[core task, frequency, main use case]`
3. **Physical / Environment & AU Compatibility Constraints:** `[dimensions, surfaces/lighting/ports, 230V/Type I plug/RCM needs]`
4. **Hard Must-Haves (Pass/Fail):** `[non-negotiable capabilities or compatibility]`
5. **Hard Deal-Breakers (Auto-Exclude):** `[features, subscriptions, lock-in, or risks to reject]`
6. **Prioritised Nice-to-Haves (Max 3):** `[ranked trade-off preferences]`
7. **Budget Ceiling (AUD, Landed):** `[hard maximum including GST, postcode delivery, and essential accessories]`
8. **Delivery Postcode & Purchase Window:** `[AU postcode; buy-now versus wait-for-sale]`
9. **Retailer & Import Risk Tolerance:** `[AU authorised retailers only / parallel import acceptable]`
10. **Warranty, Support & Consumables Priority:** `[expected lifespan, local spare parts, ongoing costs]`
11. **Preferred Sources & Category Cautions:** `[priority outlets; known SKU or variant traps]`
12. **Current Stage & Optional Spreadsheet / Reset Note:** `[Stage 0–5; spreadsheet link; fresh-chat note]`
