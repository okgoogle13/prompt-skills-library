# Australian Consumer & Offer Screening Checks

> General buyer-screening reference only—not legal advice. Confirm current rules directly with the ACCC, EESS, or the relevant state or territory consumer agency before acting on a dispute.

## Pricing and landed cost

- Report AUD landed cost, including GST, unavoidable fees, and postcode-dependent delivery.
- Verify the live checkout total on the intended purchase day.
- Consumer headline prices must display the minimum single total price, including GST and unavoidable or pre-selected fees, at least as prominently as component prices.
- Headline listings may lawfully omit delivery where free click-and-collect is available or postcode-dependent shipping cannot yet be quantified; always verify the live checkout total.

## Consumer guarantees and warranties

- Australian Consumer Law consumer guarantees are separate from manufacturer or extended warranties.
- Consumer guarantees cannot generally be excluded and may outlast a manufacturer warranty.
- Acceptable quality depends on price, product type, age, claims, and use; there is no fixed statutory lifespan.
- Distinguish seller remedies under consumer guarantees from manufacturer warranty support.
- For marketplace purchases, identify the actual third-party seller and distinguish private sellers from businesses.

## Parallel imports and marketplaces

- Flag unauthorised sellers, overseas dispatch, non-AU plugs or voltage, and missing RCM marking.
- Parallel-import sellers may still owe consumer guarantees, but local manufacturer warranty, spare-parts, and support arrangements can differ.
- Do not assume a local manufacturer will honour an excluded warranty or supply local parts.

## Electrical safety

- For in-scope household low-voltage electrical equipment, check for the Regulatory Compliance Mark (RCM) and authorised Australian supply arrangements.
- Treat a missing RCM mark, incompatible plug or voltage, or unclear importer identity as a purchase-risk flag.

## Return and support checks

- Record the retailer’s return window, return shipping responsibility, restocking fees, and exclusions.
- Record manufacturer warranty duration, claim process, local service availability, and consumable or spare-part availability.
- For purchases involving ongoing costs, record filters, bags, batteries, subscriptions, mounting hardware, or required accessories.
