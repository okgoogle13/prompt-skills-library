# Product-Evidence Policy

This expands `shopping-core.md`. It is the buyer’s chosen evidence policy, not a claim that excluded sources are dishonest.

## Preferred sources

Check preferred sources listed in Field 11 of `purchase-brief.md` first when relevant. For example, check Vacuum Wars first for robot vacuums, but apply the same article-level acquisition, sponsorship, affiliate-disclosure, and methodology screening without exemption.

## Exclusions from performance judgements

Exclude from performance judgements:

- Sponsored or paid-placement reviews.
- Reviews based on gifted, free-sample, or loaned products.
- Manufacturer-written material.
- Promotional shopping articles and marketing testimonials.

## Quarantine rule

Quarantine any review whose sponsorship or product-acquisition status cannot be established. Quarantined sources may be logged in the Source Screening Register for transparency, but must not be used to establish performance claims.

## Affiliate funding

Affiliate funding alone is not an exclusion. Disclose it, verify self-purchase or editorial policy and article-level exceptions, then assess the review method and actual evidence.

## Source roles

- Manufacturer and manual sources may establish advertised specifications.
- Seller sources may establish offers and transaction terms.
- Neither independently establishes superior real-world performance.

## Evidence categories

Keep these categories separate:

1. Measured results
2. Qualitative observations
3. Advertised specifications
4. Owner feedback
5. Seller offers
6. Analyst judgement

## Guardrails

- Missing evidence means `Unknown`, not poor performance.
- Do not transfer test results or prices between similar model variants.
- Do not compare incompatible scores as if they share a scale.
- Do not invent precise composite rankings.
- Do not infer failure rates from complaint forums.
- Do not treat multiple articles repeating one announcement as independent corroboration.
- Treat self-critique or second-model review as an error-finding aid, not independent verification.
